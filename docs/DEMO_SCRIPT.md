# SS Healthcare Admin OS — 8-Minute Client Demo Script

## 0:00–0:45 — Opening

"This is SS Healthcare's internal Admin OS. It is not replacing your existing website. It is the operations layer for inquiries, calls, staff, attendance, clients, and billing."

## 0:45–1:30 — Dashboard

Show `/admin`.

Talk track:
"The dashboard gives management a live operational snapshot: leads, call inquiries, staff, attendance, and revenue movement."

## 1:30–3:00 — Call Leads

Show `/admin/calls`.

Talk track:
"Every manual phone inquiry can land here. Staff can review call logs, summary, intent, captured lead data, and transcript. Only qualified inquiries are pushed into the CRM."

Click: View transcript.
Click: Add to CRM.

## 3:00–4:30 — AI CRM Pipeline

Show `/admin/crm`.

Talk track:
"Once added, the lead moves through a structured healthcare sales pipeline: New Lead, New Inquiry, In Discussion, Quotation Sent, Form Submitted, Staff Assigned, Deposit Pending, Active Client, Monthly Billing, and Closed Won."

Move one card or show stages.

## 4:30–5:30 — Staff Assignment

Open lead card or assignment modal.

Talk track:
"At the Staff Assigned stage, all available staff can be reviewed and assigned. This creates a traceable assignment history and moves the client forward operationally."

## 5:30–6:20 — Template Messages

Click template action.

Talk track:
"Today this logs the message action and can open WhatsApp. In production, this connects to Meta WhatsApp Business API for approved template delivery and status tracking."

## 6:20–7:10 — HR and Attendance

Show `/admin/hr` and `/admin/attendance`.

Talk track:
"The HR layer keeps employees, availability, ID cards, and attendance connected to the CRM workflow. This becomes the basis for payroll and monthly billing."

## 7:10–8:00 — Close

"The advantage is that staff can keep working naturally. Calls stay manual, but the data becomes structured, trackable, and convertible into clients. Callyzer and Meta are integration layers that plug into this workflow when ready."
