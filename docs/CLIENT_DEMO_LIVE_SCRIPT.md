# Client Demo Script — Live Operational Version

Use this exact framing:

> This is not a static CRM screen. Your employees keep answering calls normally. Callyzer sends the call records into this admin OS, the team reviews them, and only qualified calls become pipeline leads.

Demo sequence:

1. Open Dashboard and show live counts.
2. Open System Status to prove backend and database are connected.
3. Open Callyzer Calls and show received call log.
4. Review a call, add patient/service/location notes.
5. Upload recording only if the call matters.
6. Click Add to Pipeline.
7. Open AI CRM and show the new lead in New Lead stage.
8. Move the lead to In Discussion or Quotation Sent.
9. Open Attendance and mark one employee present.
10. Return to Dashboard and show updated attendance/pipeline data.

Do not overpromise automatic transcript until real recording upload and transcription is stable.
