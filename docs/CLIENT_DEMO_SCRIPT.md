# Client Demo Script

We are not changing how your staff answers calls. Your employees continue answering phone calls normally.

Callyzer tracks those calls and sends the call activity into the CRM. The CRM then creates a structured Call Review Inbox where your team can see caller number, employee name, call time, duration, service need, location, notes, and optional recording.

The important difference is control: not every call becomes a lead automatically. Your team reviews the call first. If it is a real inquiry, they click Add to Pipeline. That creates a clean CRM lead in the AI CRM pipeline.

The flow is:

1. Employee receives a call.
2. Callyzer tracks it.
3. The CRM receives the call log.
4. Staff reviews the call.
5. Staff uploads recording only if important.
6. Staff clicks Add to Pipeline.
7. The inquiry becomes a CRM lead.

This keeps the CRM clean, reduces missed inquiries, and gives management visibility into call activity without forcing the team to change their phone workflow.
